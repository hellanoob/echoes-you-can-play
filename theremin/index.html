Echoes You Can Play · Room 1

Theremin

Petrograd, 1920

The first instrument you play without touching it. Move your hands in the air, and the history unlocks as you play.

The camera stays on this device. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Or open this page on your phone.

Archive

    The sound is made live, with no recordings. A real theremin senses your hands through two antennas, as part of its circuit. A phone or a laptop can’t do that, so the camera watches your hands instead, and how big your hand looks stands in for how close it is. The vibrato knob, the tuning aid and the range of notes are design choices for this room, not parts of the original instrument.
