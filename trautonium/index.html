    The sound is made live, with no recordings. On a Trautonium, pressing the wire down harder makes it louder. A phone screen can’t feel pressure, so here the phone lies on something soft and the way it tips stands in. On a MacBook, the trackpad can read real pressure, and pressing past the deep click adds vibrato, which is a design choice. The panel is a simplified version of a Trautonium’s, not a replica.
