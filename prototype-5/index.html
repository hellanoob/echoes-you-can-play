My own instrument, opened up. A headband, a heartbeat, a laptop and a phone, all wired into one patch. Play each part by hand, and the wiring unlocks as you play.

No sound? Check the silent switch and the volume.

Signal map

What drives what. In the performance each source is my body or my desk, and everything goes into one VCV Rack patch. In this room you play the sources by hand.

  1. Headbandraw EEG: alpha, beta, delta

    Pitch of three synth layers in a four step sequencer that never stops

    Here: the three waves drift on their own. Drag them.

  2. Headbandblink

    Rings the bell

    Here: the eyes blink on their own every few seconds. Tap them to blink more.

  3. Headbandfocus

    Delay on the bell. Focus keeps rising, so the bell smears into echoes

    Here: it rises on its own.

  4. Headbandeyebrow raise

    Brings focus down and keeps it down while held, so the delay clears

    Here: hold the eyebrows up.

  5. Apple Watchheart rate

    Tempo of everything. The drop runs at four times the tempo

    Here: tap the heart like a heartbeat. The tempo is half of what you tap, and it settles back to 60.

  6. Laptoplid angle

    Distorted bass. Wider open, more bass and more grit

    Here: drag the lid.

  7. Laptoptap and hard hit

    Tap: snare over the beat. Hard hit: distorted techno kick

    Here: the TAP and HIT pads on the box.

  8. Phoneshake

    The drop: the melody ducks, the beat stops, a fast kick fades in. Keep going and a riser builds

    Here: shake your phone, or shake the one in the room with the mouse.

  9. Beatkick and hat

    Always going under the melody. Switched on and off by hand. Stops whenever the drop is on, so the kicks never overlap

    Here: BEAT on the rack.
