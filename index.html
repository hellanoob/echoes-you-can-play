An interactive documentary · Jesse Xu

Echoes You Can Play

Three rooms of electronic instruments, from 1920 to now. Play each one with your hands, your phone or your body, and its history unlocks as you play.

  1. Room 1

    Theremin

    Petrograd, 1920

    Played without touching it. The camera watches your hands in the air.

    Camera · hands

  2. Room 2

    Trautonium

    Berlin, 1930

    Press a wire and slide along it. The phone’s tilt stands in for pressure.

    Touch · tilt

  3. Room 3

    Prototype 5

    Toronto, 2026

    My own instrument: a headband, a heartbeat, a laptop and a phone, wired into one patch.

    Tap · drag · shake

Each room starts with a tap and has its own sound. Exit, in the corner of every room, brings you back here.
